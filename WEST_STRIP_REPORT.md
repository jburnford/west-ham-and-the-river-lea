4 October 2026, branch `west-strip`, worktree `/home/jic823/book_website-weststrip`. Parent session, working directly (the author chose a hybrid round: agents T18 and T19 for roads and unfinished sites, the parent for the west-bank strip).

## What changed and why

The author asked for the core Lower Lea, from Bromley gasworks to the East London Soap Works and from the Woolwich branch west to the factory strip on the west bank of the Lee Navigation, with gaps filled. The largest gap was ten industrial sites on the west bank from Imperial Street to Four Mills, excluded earlier as "outside the western riverside corridor", plus one unnamed petroleum store. All are now modelled, one building per outline of the author's 1891–96 OS GeoPackage, with names, uses, storeys and construction read from Goad fire insurance plans.

Each adds one line to `group_registers` in `build_factory_buildings.py`.
