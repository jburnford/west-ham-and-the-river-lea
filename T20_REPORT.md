## What changed and why

### New register `data/maps/railway-levels.json`
- `datum`: the project conversion (scene y = (ft − 1.3) × 0.3048 − 1.83544).
- `railStack`: rail top = formation + 0.46 m (the renderer's box stack; same as `east-depot-junctions.json`). A rail reading sets formation = reading − 0.46; a bank-top reading sets formation = reading.
- `atGrade`: where the OS draws no hatching, formation = OS ground reading beside the line + 0.1 m (ballast top 0.34 m and rail top 0.56 m above the ground). **Explicit estimate.**
- `embankment`: crest half-width 4.5 m, side slope 1 in 1.75, measured on the OS hatching (toes about ±10 m on the LT&SR east bank, about 9.5 m south of the line by the gas works).
- Per railway: `profile` ([chainage, formation, basis]), `controls` (every spot height used or checked: id, chainage, offset, reading, scene level, kind, use, evidence), `segments` (what is measured and what is interpolated), and for the LT&SR `route`/`priorRoute`/`routeEvidence`, the junction and the `ltsr-manor-road` bridge. `unchanged` records the railways outside the register and why (main line, northern connection, North London).
- Generated by a scratch script (`t20/gen_register.py`) that projects the spot heights onto the routes; the check re-reads `reference/spot-heights/heights.geojson` and fails if any copy drifts.
