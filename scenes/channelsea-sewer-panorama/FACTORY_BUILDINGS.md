## Evidence and reconstruction decisions

- **Howards:** 86 ranges cover the northern process houses, warehouse, salts,
  ether and acid departments, old mill, four Quinine parts, mercurial/potash
  works, southern warehouses, cocaine department and offices. Numbered map
  references are retained. The four more detailed Quinine polygons supersede
  their rectangular envelopes. The original
  [Quinine research register](../../data/maps/city-mills-building-register.json)
  is retained as source history; its initial null elevations are not the current
  model settings. The old mill has a pale upper exterior and a taller roof,
  informed by the supplied photographs. Individual roofs have not all been
  identified in those photographs.
  The September footprint pass now aligns 83 ranges to 101 source polygons in
  53 groups, retaining those interpreted internal compartments and elevations.
  Old mill 644 uses a direct OS trace; shed 524 retains its local Goad relationship
  to process house 520; shed 620 remains deferred. Two chimney bases are mapped
  independently and sixteen move with their reviewed departments. The adjacent
  bank is reconciled locally while preserving the millrace. See
  [the footprint continuation](FOOTPRINT_ALIGNMENT.md#howards-continuation-recovered-29-september-2026).
- **Sugar House:** Goad F3 reference 570 is the tall building in Chippendale's
  cooperage. Its mapped position is registered separately from the estimated
  elevation. [ASE report 2013200](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/catalogue/adsdata/arch-480-1/dissemination/pdf/archaeol6-159592_1.pdf),
  section 6.15 and Figure 21, supports the 1882 date, five-storey red-brick form
  and paired east–west gables. This replaces a low generic range. The neighbouring
  Building 15/17/17a additions remain bracketed between the 1894 and 1916 maps;
  their exact construction year has not been resolved and their later forms are
  not silently copied into 1900.
- **Three Mills:** the House Mill, Clock Mill, paired drying kilns, turret,
  waterside office/cottage ranges, bonded wharf warehouse and distillery ranges
  have separate records. The former standalone mill models are replaced to
  avoid duplicate volumes. House Mill boarding, dormers and Clock Mill's conical
  kilns/tower distinguish the landmarks. The modern rear of the Miller's House
  is not used. Goad explicitly marks part of the distillery as drawn from
  outside observation; its internal divisions are consequently less certain.
- **Gasworks:** nine Bromley circles from the 1893 OS replace the earlier seven,
  while the six OS-registered West Ham holders remain. The production halls,
  ancillary ranges, purifier banks and circular apparatus are now represented.
  The [Historic England holder record](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1080995)
  supports the two-tier period treatment. Later third tiers and the 1916 rail
  connection are excluded. Numbering of the lost Nos. 3 and 5 is provisional;
  the nine mapped positions are distinct from that numbering assumption.
- **Western edge:** full East London Soap Works and New Imperial Saw Mills
  coverage extends beyond the old scene clip. The
  [western context extract](../../data/maps/factory-west-context.json) preserves
  the original GIS continuation of the Old Lea and adjoining branches, plus
  the full yards. The river-relief generator now includes those continuations.

Main plan sources are the July 1893 Goad

## Accuracy and geometry

This completes mapped factory coverage, not a measured architectural survey.
Most traces are rectangular range envelopes with small recesses simplified.
The full original GIS west of the former crop was also checked for additional factories;
those outside the Old Lea/main-line boundaries are recorded as exclusions.
OS/Goad registration and the supplied GIS shoreline differ locally. Fits,
explicit placement offsets and the rejected wider City Mills fit remain in the
register. Fit residuals are internal consistency measurements, not independent
absolute accuracy. Windows, metre heights and ordinary roof pitches are estimates
unless a specific source is recorded. The [chimney survey](FACTORY_CHIMNEYS.md)
now records 54 Goad source symbols, with 19 adjacent height readings in feet,
plus a provisional Bromley boiler stack and 24 interpreted retort-house roof flues informed by the 1924 aerials (79 stacks in total); remaining
shaft heights and detailed profiles are estimates.

Raw floor marks are preserved separately. In particular, `1=2` has not been
silently decoded as a uniform two-storey building: the model uses an explicit
intermediate height pending a verified symbol key. Goad insurance divisions
also do not prove independent roof ridges.

Rounded adjoining envelopes are partitioned once in the rendered geometry,
with taller ranges taking precedence. Original envelopes remain available.
Explicitly reviewed small shoreline excesses are trimmed to the existing GIS
bank, and holder rings are kept clear. Changes in area are recorded in the
local geometry audit. Four named mill/race relationships retain intentional
water overlaps; ordinary factories do not obstruct the channels. Concave
footprints and holes are triangulated and roof planes clipped to their range.
The scene batches factory surfaces by material to limit extra draw calls.
