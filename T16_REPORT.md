## 2. `check_factory_buildings.py` and `stack-865-boiler-house`

### Why it failed

The record fails two assertions, one after the other.

1. **Position source (line 65).** The check requires every chimney to have a `pixelPosition` (a symbol traced on a sheet) or a `parentBuildingId`. T6 registered this stack differently. It is placed directly in world coordinates on GeoPackage fid 1061509, the small square drawn open inside range 24851, and it carries `sourceFootprintFid` and `sourcePolygons`. Neither accepted form can honestly be supplied:
   - **A parent will not work.** The builder would recompute x,z from a `localPosition`, and the check requires the parent's render polygon to *contain* the centre. The boiler-house range keeps that square as a hole (`worldHoles`), so the centre is deliberately outside the parent.
   - **A pixel position would be invented.** The stack was never traced on a sheet, and the builder would then overwrite x,z from the made-up pixel.

   **So the check's assumption is what is wrong for this registration.** Locating a stack on a supplied OS outline is at least as well sourced as a traced pixel. 33 other chimneys already carry `sourceFootprintFid`, but they came in through Goad pixel positions and were corrected later.

2. **Evidence class (the next line).** A chimney without an interpretation `evidenceType` must carry a Goad `symbolKey`. This one has neither, so it would have failed with `KeyError: 'symbolKey'`. **Here the record is what is malformed.** It is a map interpretation: T6's own `positionEvidence` says the OS labels no chimney, and the record has no Goad symbol. It just did not declare that, as the 8 existing `map-interpretation` stacks do.

### Fix

- **Record.** I added `evidenceType: "map-interpretation"` and `supportingSources: ["author-os-footprints-1891-96", "os-1893"]`. Both keys exist in `factory['sources']`: the supplied outlines, and the OS sheet the record already names as `source`. No geometry or other text changed.
- **Check.** It now accepts `sourceFootprintFid` + `sourcePolygons` as a position source. When that is the *only* source, it asserts that the stack centre lies inside the outline (with a 0.05 m tolerance). A comment in the check explains why. The 33 older chimneys are unaffected, because they still pass through `pixelPosition`.
- **Negative tests** (scratch `negtest.py`):
  - moving the stack 3 m off its square fails with `Chimney off its mapped base`;
  - removing `evidenceType` fails again with `KeyError: 'symbolKey'`;
  - the unmodified data passes.
- **The renderer reads neither field** (I grepped `docs/*.js`), so the scene is unaffected.
